Last updated: September 11, 2026

Arclo ("Arclo," "we," "us," or "our") is a web design and digital marketing agency. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website and our advertising, why we collect it, and how we handle it. By submitting your information to us or using this site, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

When you respond to our Facebook lead ads or submit a contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business name
  • Your answers to a few qualifying questions about your business

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to contact you about our website and customer-capture services, answer your questions, and schedule a consultation call. We don't use your information for any purpose unrelated to responding to your inquiry and providing our services.

Information Sharing

We don't sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information as necessary to operate our business and deliver our services, including:

  • With Meta's (Facebook and Instagram) advertising platform, in connection with the ad you responded to

These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function for us and aren't permitted to use it for their own independent purposes.

Meta Pixel & Analytics

We use the Meta Pixel on this site. The Meta Pixel lets us measure the effectiveness of our advertising and understand how visitors interact with our site, which may involve Meta collecting information about your visit in accordance with its own data practices. You can review Meta's Data Policy or manage your ad preferences directly through your Facebook or Instagram account settings.

Data Retention

We retain the personal information you provide for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, provide our services, and maintain our business records, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us
